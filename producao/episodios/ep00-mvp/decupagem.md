## PLANOS DETALHADOS

### Plano 1 — A ladeira respira
- **Keyframe (EN):** `Empty colonial hillside street at night, warm street lanterns, full moon, scattered confetti on cobblestones beginning to float upward against gravity. Wide shot. Scene emotion: quiet wonder.`
- **Motion:** `Confetti slowly floats upward, lantern light flickers gently. Camera: gentle push-in. No cuts.`
- **Áudio:** silêncio + grilos; NARRAÇÃO: *"Em Olinda, os mais velhos contam..."*

### Plano 2 — A sombra
- **Keyframe (EN):** `Same street, a HUGE mysterious shadow gliding across the facades between two colonial houses, source unseen. Low wide angle. Scene emotion: mystery (not fear).`
- **Motion:** `The giant shadow slides slowly across the wall, left to right. Camera: static. No cuts.`
- **Áudio:** NARRAÇÃO: *"...que à meia-noite, quando a última janela se apaga... a cidade acorda."* + primeira nota de frevo distante

### Plano 3 — Calu na janela
- **Keyframe (EN):** `CALU peeking out of an opened colonial window at night, wide curious eyes, moonlight on his face. Medium close-up from street level. Scene emotion: curiosity.`
- **Motion:** `The boy leans out of the window, looks left then right. Camera: static. No cuts.`
- **Áudio:** NARRAÇÃO: *"Calu nunca acreditou nas histórias."*

### Plano 4 — A descida
- **Keyframe (EN):** `CALU tiptoeing down the cobblestone street at night, following floating confetti, body leaning forward with sneaky exaggerated steps. Wide shot. Scene emotion: playful adventure.`
- **Motion:** `The boy tiptoes downhill following floating confetti. Camera: slow lateral tracking. No cuts.`
- **Áudio:** NARRAÇÃO: *"Mas naquela noite... a música veio procurar por ele."* + frevo um pouco mais perto

### Plano 5 — Tico e Chico (gag)
- **Keyframe (EN):** `TICO stepping out of a narrow alley hugging his mamulengo puppet CHICO; the puppet's head is turned on its own toward floating confetti while Tico looks at the puppet suspiciously. Medium shot. Scene emotion: comedy.`
- **Motion:** `The puppet's head slowly rotates; the boy's eyes dart between puppet and confetti. Camera: static. No cuts.`
- **Áudio:** SFX cômico de madeirinha girando; reação muda de Tico ("hã?")

### Plano 6 — A subida juntos
- **Keyframe (EN):** `CALU and TICO (carrying CHICO) climbing the winding street side by side, golden lantern light growing warmer uphill, church silhouette at the top. Wide shot from behind. Scene emotion: adventure.`
- **Motion:** `The two boys walk uphill away from camera. Camera: slow follow. No cuts.`
- **Áudio:** frevo crescendo; passos no paralelepípedo

### Plano 7 — Sombras que dançam
- **Keyframe (EN):** `Colonial house facades with GIANT dancing shadows of carnival puppets projected on the walls — but the street itself is empty. Boys' silhouettes small in the foreground, looking up. Wide shot. Scene emotion: awe.`
- **Motion:** `Giant shadows dance gently on the walls in frevo rhythm. Camera: static. No cuts.`
- **Áudio:** NARRAÇÃO: *"E quanto mais alto eles subiam... maior ficava o segredo."*

### Plano 8 — O gigante imóvel
- **Keyframe (EN):** `Alto da Sé square at night: the HOMEM DA MEIA-NOITE stands motionless in the center, BACK to camera, towering against the full moon; the two small boys frozen at the edge of the square. Extreme wide shot. Scene emotion: suspense (gentle).`
- **Motion:** `Everything still; only confetti drifts and the boys breathe. Camera: very slow push-in. No cuts.`
- **Áudio:** o frevo CESSA; silêncio; um sino badala UMA vez

### Plano 9 — O despertar
- **Keyframe (EN):** `Close-up of the HOMEM DA MEIA-NOITE's face in profile, eyes just LIGHTING UP like two warm lanterns, top hat rising slightly. Scene emotion: magical awakening.`
- **Motion:** `Eyes glow up gradually, hat lifts a few centimeters, head straightens with dignity. Camera: static. No cuts.`
- **Áudio:** SFX mágico suave (partículas douradas) + nota grave sustentada

### Plano 10 — A virada
- **Keyframe (EN):** `The HOMEM DA MEIA-NOITE mid-turn, rotating slowly toward camera with slow almost-century-old dignity, golden particles around him. Low angle medium-wide shot. Scene emotion: majesty.`
- **Motion:** `The giant completes his turn to face the camera, very slow and graceful. Camera: static low angle. No cuts.`
- **Áudio:** ranger suave de madeira/papel machê; coração batendo (sutil)

### Plano 11 — Calu sorri
- **Keyframe (EN):** `Counter-shot: CALU looking up at something enormous off-screen, moonlight and golden glow on his face — and instead of fear, a huge wondering SMILE grows. Close-up. Scene emotion: pure wonder.`
- **Motion:** `The boy's expression shifts from surprise into a wide bright smile. Camera: gentle push-in. No cuts.`
- **Áudio:** silêncio absoluto (o plano inteiro segura a respiração)

### Plano 12 — A pergunta
- **Keyframe (EN):** `Extreme wide shot: the HOMEM DA MEIA-NOITE gently leaning down toward the two tiny boys, immense and kind against the full moon over Alto da Sé. Scene emotion: the beginning of everything.`
- **Motion:** `The giant leans down slowly, gently. Camera: static. No cuts.`
- **Áudio:** HOMEM DA MEIA-NOITE (V.O., grave e caloroso): *"Você consegue ver a gente?"* → corte para preto → 1 batida de frevo → cartela **OLINDA ENCANTADA**

## LISTA DE GERAÇÃO (ordem de produção)

1. Cenários-base: Ladeira (ang. A/B/C + beco + janela de casario) e Alto da Sé (ang. A/B) — 7 imagens
2. Keyframes: 12 (4 variações cada via MCP → escolher 1) 
3. Vídeos: 12 clipes de 5–10s (**só sobre keyframes aprovados**)
4. Narração: 6 falas de narrador + 1 fala do HMN (ElevenLabs)
5. Música: 1 frevo instrumental suave ~100s com curva (distante → cresce → cessa → batida final) — Suno
6. SFX: grilos, sino, madeirinha, partículas, passos

- Corte 2 (gag): plano 5 isolado em loop (~15s) — Chico vivo?
- Corte 3 (clímax): planos 9–12 (~35s) — o despertar completo
